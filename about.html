About Readwise

A small site about a large skill.

Reading sits underneath every other kind of learning. We think it deserves to be taught on its own terms, clearly and without gimmicks.

Readwise began as a set of notes for students who were working hard and remembering little. The pattern was always the same. They read every assigned page, highlighted most of it, and could recall almost none of it a fortnight later. What helped was never a clever trick. It was a handful of plain habits: read a little every day, ask a question before you start, close the book and recall, look back a week later.

Those habits are what this site teaches. We publish two full guides and three small tools, and we would rather keep those accurate than add pages for the sake of it.

What we hold ourselves to

01

Evidence before enthusiasm

Where a claim rests on research into reading and memory, we say so in ordinary words. Where it is only our experience, we say that too. We do not promise to triple anyone’s reading speed.

02

Nothing to buy

Readwise sells no courses, apps or books. The tools are free, run in your browser and keep nothing you type. Any advertising is kept clearly separate from the guides.

03

Corrections welcome

If something here is wrong or unclear, tell us. We fix errors promptly and note meaningful changes on the page, as set out in our Editorial Policy.
